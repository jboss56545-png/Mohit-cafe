Our Story

More than
just coffee.

Tucked into a quiet lane in Assi, Mohit's Cafe is the kind of place you find by accident and return to on purpose.

Morning light, the hum of the city below, a properly pulled coffee and food made fresh when you order it. Come for breakfast, stay for a pizza, and let the afternoon slow down.

Whether you're a local, a traveller or just passing through, there's a seat with your name on it. No rush. No fuss. Just good coffee and better company.
